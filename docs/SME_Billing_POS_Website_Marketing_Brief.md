## 1. One-line product

**BillPOS turns messy on-site SME billing (handwritten slips, WhatsApp chaos, delayed CA invoices) into professional GST-ready invoices — from Telegram, phone, or photo — in under two minutes.**

---

## 2. Problem (the job to be done)

### Who hurts
- **Boutique hotels, villas, farmstays, B&Bs** — manager writes F&B / extras on paper at checkout; guest questions totals; CA invoices days later.
- **Cafés, cloud kitchens, small restaurants** — need formal bills without Petpooja-class cost and training.
- **Salons, spas, clinics, tuition centres, workshops** — service + product mix, cash/UPI/card at counter, weak paper trail.
- **Owner-operated SMEs (India-first)** — ₹50L–₹5Cr annual revenue; cannot justify full ERP/PMS.

### Pain today
1. Handwritten bills lack **sanctity** → guests negotiate, dispute, delay payment.  
2. Formal software is **expensive, multi-user, overbuilt** for 1–2 staff.  
3. Owner wants **GST-looking invoices** without hiring someone to retype everything.  
4. Staff already live in **WhatsApp / Telegram** — not another desktop POS.  
5. Photo of a bill pad exists; **nothing turns it into a locked professional PDF**.

### Emotional job
> “When the guest is leaving, I need something that looks official so I don’t argue about numbers — and I need it *now*, on my phone.”

---

## 3. Solution (what we sell)

**BillPOS** is a **Telegram-first + web** billing system for SMEs:

| Capability | What the user experiences |
|------------|---------------------------|
| **Type a bill** | Guest, location/unit, line items → draft with official rates |
| **Photo OCR** | Snap handwritten pad → AI reads lines → maps to price list |
| **Locked catalog** | Menu / rate card is source of truth — no ad-hoc discounting on matched items |
| **Professional tax invoice PDF** | GSTIN, HSN, CGST/SGST, bank details, logo, declaration |
| **Stored + versioned** | Every invoice saved; edits archive prior PDF (audit-friendly) |
| **Mark paid** | UPI / cash / card in chat or web |
| **Share** | PDF to guest / owner; public invoice link |

**Not** a full hotel PMS, channel manager, or inventory ERP.  
**Is** the missing “checkout sanctity layer” for SMEs.

**Title:** Paper bills cost you more than ink.  
Bullets:

- Manager retypes for CA next week  
- Rates drift from the menu

### 9.3 How it works (3 steps)
1. **Capture** — Type in Telegram or photograph the bill pad.  
2. **Lock** — Items match your official rate card; 5% GST (or your slab) applied correctly.  
3. **Issue** — Tax invoice PDF with your logo & GSTIN; stored and versioned if edited.

### 9.4 Hospitality deep-dive
**Title:** Built for the morning of checkout.  
Copy: Villa / room extras, F&B packages, high tea, bonfire, spa — billed while the guest is still at the property. Room stay can stay with your CA; BillPOS owns **site extras**.  
**Micro-proof:** “Proven on a 4-villa design estate near Delhi-NCR.”

### 9.5 Multi-vertical
**Title:** Same engine. Your catalog.

- Hotels & villas  
- Cafés & restaurants  
- Salons & spas  
- Clinics & studios  
- Events & rentals  

Each line: *“Upload rate card → staff bills from Telegram.”*

- Supabase storage of every PDF

### 9.7 Sample invoice callout
Full-width screenshot of tax invoice (blur real guest if needed).  
Caption: *Looks like Tally. Issued like chat.*

### 9.8 Pricing teaser

## 10. Design system direction (for site builder)

### Aesthetic
- **Hospitality-calm meets finance-serious**  
- Palette: deep forest green `#1E3D26` / `#2F5D3A`, warm paper `#F4F1EA`, ink `#1C2A1F`, soft gold accent `#B8892A`  
- Typography: distinctive serif or high-quality display for headlines (not Inter-only); clean sans for body  
- Photography: real properties, manager on phone, paper → PDF transition — not generic “AI network”  
- Motion: subtle, 200–300ms; no bounce  

### Anti-AI-slop checklist
- [ ] No purple gradient SaaS hero  
- [ ] No 3 equal feature cards with lucide icons as the whole story  
- [ ] No “Our mission is to empower…” without product  
- [ ] No fake 4.9★ from 12,000 customers  
- [ ] Show **real UI**: Telegram + invoice PDF  

### Logo
- Product logo separate from client logos  
- Support white-label invoice logo per customer (property logo on PDF)
