## 1. One-line pitch

**BillBanaoPay** lets small businesses create **professional invoices from Telegram or a photo of a handwritten slip** — with optional GST, stored PDFs, payment tracking, and an owner login to see collections.

---

## 2. Who it is for (generic — not industry-locked)

Any **store, shop, workshop, service provider, or small business** that:

- Issues bills at the counter or on-site  
- Still uses paper pads, WhatsApp notes, or Excel  
- Wants invoices that look **formal** so customers pay without arguing  
- Has 1–10 staff, not a full accounts team on site  
- Needs **optional GST** on some bills and not on others  

**Examples of customers (illustrative only — not industry silos):**  
Kirana + services counter, boutique shop, guest house extras desk, tuition centre, salon, repair shop, rental provider, café takeaway, freelance studio.

---

## 3. Problem (customer language)

> “My staff write bills on a pad. When I send a neat bill, people pay. When it looks casual, they negotiate. Software is either too expensive or too heavy for us.”

| Pain | Result |
|------|--------|
| Handwritten bills | No sanctity; disputes; weak records |
| Delayed formal invoices | Cash leakage; messy CA month-end |
| One GST setting for everything | Wrong bills when some sales are exempt / non-GST |
| No single place for “what came in” | Owner flies blind on collections |
| Desktop POS for 1 phone user | Training cost, unused seats |

---

## 4. Solution

**BillBanaoPay** is a **phone-first billing platform**:

1. Staff **type a bill** in Telegram **or photograph** a handwritten slip (AI OCR).  
2. Lines match the **business price list** (official rates).  
3. Staff **choose GST ON or OFF** (5% as CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5% when ON).  
4. System issues a **professional invoice PDF** (logo, GSTIN when used, totals, paid status).  
5. PDF is **stored**; edits keep **version history**.  
6. **Owner / admin login** shows all invoices and payments received.

## 6. Core features (market product)

### A. Billing capture
- Telegram bot for staff (allowlisted numbers)  
- Free-text bill entry  
- Photo OCR of handwritten or printed slips  
- Web app for staff who prefer browser  

### B. Price list (catalog)
- Your items and rates  
- Smart matching (aliases for how staff write items)  
- Official rate preferred when item matches catalog  

### C. GST control (per invoice)
- **Include GST** — 5% (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST) on taxable lines  
- **Exclude GST** — bill without tax (common for many SME cases)  
- Visible toggle in **Telegram before confirm**  
- Invoice PDF reflects the choice  

### D. Professional invoice
- Business name, address, GSTIN (when configured)  
- Item table, totals, amount in words  
- Optional tax breakup  
- Shareable PDF + web invoice link  
- Logo support  

### E. Payments
- Mark paid: UPI / cash / card  
- Owner sees collected vs pending  

### F. Admin / owner portal
- Secure PIN login (Owner / Admin)  
- List of all invoices  
- Filters: paid / unpaid / void  
- Totals: collections and pending  
- Open invoice or download PDF  

### G. Corrections
- Edit invoice after issue  
- Previous version archived (history + old PDF)  
- Re-issue PDF

## 10. FAQ (prospective customer questions)

### Product basics
**Q: What is BillBanaoPay?**  
A: A simple billing system for small businesses. Staff create invoices from Telegram or a photo; owners see payments online.

**Q: Who makes it?**  
A: OmniDEL.ai.

**Q: Do I need a computer at the counter?**  
A: No. Phone + Telegram is enough. Web is available as backup.

**Q: Does it work offline?**  
A: Creating invoices needs internet. Drafts live on the server once sent.

### Billing & GST
**Q: Can I issue bills without GST?**  
A: Yes. Every draft has a **GST ON / OFF** control. When OFF, the invoice has no CGST/SGST.

**Q: What does GST ON mean?**  
A: Standard 5% on the taxable amount, shown as 2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST (configurable for your CA’s guidance).

**Q: Can GST % be different from 5%?**  
A: Platform starts with 5% as the common case; other slabs can be configured per customer.

**Q: Can staff change prices freely?**  
A: Items that match your official price list use catalog rates so prices stay consistent.

**Q: What if an item is not on the list?**  
A: Staff can add a custom line with amount; you can add it to the catalog later.

### Telegram & OCR
**Q: Why Telegram?**  
A: Staff already use chat apps. No new desktop software to learn.

**Q: How does photo billing work?**  
A: Staff photograph a handwritten pad; AI reads guest, items, and quantities; you confirm before issue.

**Q: What if OCR is wrong?**  
A: You see a draft first. Edit or retype, then confirm. Nothing is final until confirm.

**Q: Is WhatsApp supported?**  
A: Product path today is Telegram; WhatsApp can be a roadmap item.

### Invoices & records
**Q: What does the invoice look like?**  
A: Formal PDF — business details, line items, totals, optional tax, payment status. Suitable to share with customers.

**Q: Where are invoices stored?**  
A: Securely in the cloud; each PDF is saved and linked to the invoice record.

**Q: Can I edit after sending?**  
A: Yes. Edits create a new version; older version is kept in history.

**Q: Can I get my data for my CA?**  
A: Yes — invoice list, PDFs, and paid status support month-end accounting.

### Owner / admin
**Q: How do I see money received?**  
A: Owner / Admin login shows collected (paid) vs pending, and every invoice.

**Q: Separate logins for staff and owner?**  
A: Yes. Staff create bills; Owner sees all invoices and payments.

**Q: Who can use the Telegram bot?**  
A: Only allowlisted phone accounts you approve.

### Security & setup
**Q: How long to go live?**  
A: Catalog + branding + bot allowlist — typically a short pilot setup.

**Q: Do you train staff?**  
A: Simple commands: type bill, photo, confirm, paid. Most learn in one session.

**Q: Multi-branch?**  
A: Roadmap / multi-outlet packaging; start with one outlet.

**Q: Pricing?**  
A: SME-friendly subscription; pilot available. Contact OmniDEL.ai for current plans.

**Q: Is this a full accounting software?**  
A: No. It is billing + collections visibility. It complements Tally/your CA — it does not replace full books.

**Q: Languages?**  
A: Product UI English; invoices are numeric and work for any language of business.
